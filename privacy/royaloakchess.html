Royal Oak Chess Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Royal Oak Chess is designed to work without an account. Greyscale Holding LLC does not operate any server that receives your games or personal information.

Data stored on your device

Games in progress, lesson progress, your hint balance, and your app settings are stored locally on your iPhone. This information is used only to provide the app's features and is never sent to us.

Advertising

Royal Oak Chess offers an optional "watch an ad for a hint" reward. These ads are served by Google AdMob. When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process device and usage information — including a device advertising identifier, coarse location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction events — in order to deliver and measure the ad. This processing is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Before the first ad request, the app asks for your permission to track using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not made available to AdMob and you will receive non-personalized advertising. You can change this answer at any time in iPhone Settings under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Purchasing any hint pack or Unlimited Hints removes the need to watch ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The app receives purchase status from StoreKit so it can unlock what you bought, but we never receive your payment-card details. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Royal Oak Chess is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

Because the app's own data is stored locally, you can remove it by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features change. The revised policy and its effective date will be posted at this URL.
