Anchor

Starting is the hardest part. Anchor makes the start smaller.

Anchor is a daily routine and focus companion built for ADHD brains. It replaces growing backlogs and punishing streaks with one visible next step and calm, direct language.

  • One-step-at-a-time morning, evening, desk-reset, and custom routines
  • Just One Thing timers and quiet body-double focus sessions
  • Leave-on-time Anchor Alarms
  • Streaks that pause instead of wiping out progress
  • Widgets, Live Activities, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, and Reduce Motion
  • No account, ads, analytics, or data collection

Coming soon to the App Store. Anchor Pro includes a 2-week free trial with weekly, monthly, and annual options.
