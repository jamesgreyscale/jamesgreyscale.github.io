Chess Strategy: Coach & Learn

See the board. Find the plan.

Most chess apps beat you and move on. Chess Strategy explains what just happened, what the position wants, and which move gets you there — while you play.

  • Guided Play with the Oakmaster Coach: Full Coach, Advisor, or Silent
  • Trainer strength from Beginner to Grand Master
  • 390 puzzles from real games across thirteen themed sets
  • Structured courses: opening principles, tactics, checkmate patterns, endgames, positional play
  • Challenge the Coach — play your move and get an honest verdict
  • Free Play for a normal game with no coaching
  • Plays fully offline — no account, no ads, no data collection
