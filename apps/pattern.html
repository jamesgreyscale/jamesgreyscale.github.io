Pattern

Notice what changes. Bring your clinician a clear record.

Pattern turns quick daily check-ins into useful symptom trends and a clean, shareable doctor report for perimenopause and menopause. It records irregular bleeding without fertility assumptions or cycle predictions.

  • Fast daily tracking for hot flashes, sleep, mood, cognition, body symptoms, and possible influences
  • 30-, 90-, and 365-day trends with careful association language
  • Monthly summaries that treat missed days without judgment
  • Doctor-ready PDF reports for 30, 90, or 180 days
  • Optional read-only sleep and heart-rate context from Apple Health
  • JSON export, complete deletion, reminders, Face ID or passcode lock, and a check-in widget

Private by design

Pattern has no account, ads, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated server. Entries and Health context stay on your iPhone. A PDF or JSON file leaves the app only through a share destination you choose.

Pattern Pro

Pattern Pro is planned at $9.99 monthly or $59.99 annually. Both plans include a 14-day free trial for eligible subscribers. The Apple purchase sheet shows exact eligibility, price, and renewal terms before confirmation. Subscriptions can be managed or canceled through your Apple Account.

Pattern is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, medication, or emergency services. Share its information with a qualified clinician.
