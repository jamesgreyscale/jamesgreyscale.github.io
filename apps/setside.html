SetSide

Know what to set aside before the quarterly deadline gets close.

SetSide brings federal tax planning, business mileage, receipt capture, and accountant-ready reporting into one calm, private iPhone app for US freelancers and 1099 workers.

  • A live federal set-aside estimate with a plain-English breakdown
  • Current-year and prior-year safe-harbor planning modes
  • Battery-conscious drive suggestions, route rules, and manual trips
  • On-device receipt recognition and familiar expense categories
  • Quarter and year reports with PDF and CSV exports
  • Local deadline reminders and Home Screen widgets

Private by design

SetSide has no app account, advertising, analytics SDK, or developer-operated server. Financial entries, receipt images, OCR results, trip records, and estimates stay on your iPhone unless you choose to export them.

SetSide Pro

SetSide Pro includes every feature. The launch price is $7.99 monthly or $49.99 annually. The annual plan includes a 14-day free trial. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled through your Apple Account before renewal.
