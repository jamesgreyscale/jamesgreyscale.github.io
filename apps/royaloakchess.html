Royal Oak Chess

Think. Plan. Conquer.

Royal Oak Chess is a beautiful, complete chess club in your pocket. Play the Oakmaster engine across ten levels, pass the phone to a friend, connect two devices over Bluetooth, or work through sixteen guided lessons that build real understanding.

  • Ten engine levels from Beginner to Grand Master
  • Two-player pass and play on a single device
  • Nearby Match between two iPhones over Bluetooth
  • Sixteen guided lessons: forks, pins, back-rank mates, stalemate, castling, en passant, and more
  • Hints that draw the best move directly on the board
  • Hand-rendered 3D pieces with an optional 2D piece set
  • Plays fully offline — no account and no sign-in
